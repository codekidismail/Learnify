# FAQ
1.
What is Learnify?
Learnify is an interactive Learning Management System (LMS) designed to make learning engaging, organized, and easy for students through courses, quizzes, badges, and progress tracking.
2.
Do I need an account to use Learnify?
Yes, you need to register or log in to access your courses, track progress, and save achievements.
3.
What can I do on the Courses page?
You can explore topics, complete modules, take quizzes, and mark lessons as complete to update your progress bar.
4.
How do badges work?
Badges are rewards given when you complete certain tasks or finish courses. They help keep you motivated and track your accomplishments.
5.
What is the Announcements Ticker?
It displays scrolling updates such as upcoming events, reminders, tips, and important messages.
6.
Is Learnify suitable for all age groups?
Yes! Learnify is designed to be simple, friendly, and useful for students of all ages. For now we only have a select grade but in the future we hope to expand this to all ages and all grades!
7.
Can I check my progress?
Yes, Learnify includes animated progress bars that update each time you finish a lesson.
8.
What makes Learnify different from other LMS platforms?
Its simplicity, smooth animations, visual progress tracking, motivational features, and creatively designed interface make learning more enjoyable.
9.
Is Learnify currently a full website or a demo?
Learnify is a sample/demo LMS showcasing how an interactive learning platform works, including essential features like courses, badges, quizzes, and announcements.
10.
Will Learnify get more features in the future??
Yes! New sections, courses, login improvements, and more interactive tools can be added as the project grows.
